1. At a glance

Coo’s Joint is a single-player Android game. Core progression is saved on the device in the app’s local save file. The game does not require a player account and does not operate a cloud-save database.

The project currently includes an AdMob integration in test mode using Google test IDs; production ad IDs are not configured. This policy describes the additional data handling that may occur if optional rewarded ads are enabled for a public release. No separate gameplay-analytics provider is currently identified in the project. If the release configuration differs, update this policy before distribution.

Important: Do not describe advertising identifiers as “non-PII.” Device identifiers, IP addresses, and usage signals can be personal data or personal information under applicable laws, even when they do not directly reveal a person’s name.

3. Information stored by the game

Local gameplay save

The game writes its save to user://coos_joint_save_v1.json in Godot’s app-specific user-data area. The save can contain gameplay progress such as Star Beans and other game currencies, business ownership, managers and upgrades, settings, statistics, story/progression flags, and save timestamps.

The game code does not send this save file to a publisher-operated server or cloud-save service. The file is used on the device to restore local progress. The game does not currently provide account-based or cross-device cloud synchronization.

You can use the in-game reset control, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove local game data. Android system backup and restore behavior can depend on device/account settings and the final Android manifest; it is not a game-managed cloud-save feature. The publisher cannot recover a local save that has been erased or lost.

Messages you send us

If you email support or privacy, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, and any diagnostic details you include. We use it to respond, troubleshoot, and keep records required for security or legal purposes. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

4. Advertising and analytics

Optional rewarded ads

If production rewarded advertising is enabled, the app may request an optional rewarded ad through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). You choose whether to start a rewarded-ad flow. Ads may be unavailable because of network, inventory, regional, or consent settings; the game does not promise that an ad will always be available.

Google’s published Android data-disclosure guidance for Google Mobile Ads describes automatic collection/sharing that can include an IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), product interactions such as app launches, taps, and video views, diagnostic information, and device/account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or App Set ID. The exact data and purposes depend on the SDK version, app configuration, ad partners, region, and user choices. These providers may use data for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

The current project uses Google test IDs and is_real=false; those are development settings, not production ad credentials. Before release, the publisher must verify the exact SDK and mediation versions and update this policy and the Play Console Data safety form to match them. If ads are removed, revise this section to describe the shipped configuration instead.

Gameplay analytics / telemetry

No separate gameplay analytics service is currently identified in the project configuration. If we add analytics or telemetry, we will update this policy to name the provider, explain the information collected and purposes, and update applicable consent and store disclosures before enabling it.

5. Device identifiers, cookies, and similar technologies

The native core game does not operate a first-party website or use browser cookies for gameplay. Advertising SDKs or ad content may use advertising/device identifiers, SDK storage, cookies, or similar technologies to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and remember privacy choices. Some identifiers or network signals may be treated as personal data under applicable law.

You can review or reset Android advertising-ID controls in your device settings and manage Google advertising settings through Google’s account tools. These controls do not replace any consent or opt-out rights that apply under local law. Where required, the production app must present an appropriate consent/privacy-choice flow before the relevant ad processing occurs and provide a way to revisit choices; the publisher must confirm that flow is implemented in the release build.

  • Advertising providers: if enabled, Google Mobile Ads and any configured mediation/ad partners may receive information from their SDKs to provide, measure, secure, or improve ad services, subject to their terms, settings, and applicable privacy choices.
  • Support: messages are used to reply and address the issue.
  • Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

Legal bases, where applicable

The legal basis depends on where you live and the final feature/SDK configuration. It may include consent for personalized advertising or non-essential storage where required, legitimate interests for support, service security, and fraud prevention, and compliance with legal obligations. The publisher must confirm the applicable basis and implement the corresponding consent or opt-out flow before enabling production ad processing.

We do not operate an account database or sell the local save file. We do not receive local save data through the game. 8. Adults and children’s privacy

Coo’s Joint is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not designed or marketed to children. The app does not currently verify a player’s age; an 18+ intended audience is not itself an age-verification mechanism. If you are under 18, do not use the game.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent personal information to us, contact the privacy email listed below; we will review the request and take appropriate steps. Before release, the publisher must make the Play Console target-audience, content-rating, and ad settings consistent with the actual game and this 18+ audience statement.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Third-party services apply their own safeguards; consult their current documentation for details.
